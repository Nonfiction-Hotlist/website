::: section about
The Nonfiction Hotlist curates and champions exceptional nonfiction storytelling, connecting creators with audiences, industry opportunities, and sustainable pathways forward.

Through thoughtful conversation, community building and curiosity we’re discovering new and unexpected ways to offer support to creators at any stage of their journey, while also joining forces with long-standing industry organizations who seek meaningful change.

::: section values
## values

- We lead with generosity, respect, and thoughtful relationship-building.

- We convene rather than gatekeep, creating spaces where storytellers, funders, and audiences engage directly with projects and each other.

- We believe collaboration within a community creates alchemy impossible to replicate, and building with a village leads to the best work.

- Everyone is invited.
